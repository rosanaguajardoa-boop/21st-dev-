## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS. Chosen because the site must take payments on the web (server routes for a checkout provider such as Stripe), it stays compatible with 21st.dev / shadcn-style React components, and it deploys cleanly to Vercel or any Node host. Revisit if the business turns out to need only a static page.

## Users

Primary: prospective customers of the owner's own business who arrive ready to evaluate and buy. The job is to understand the offer quickly and complete a purchase online.

Open: the specific audience (who they are, where they come from) is not yet defined.

## Product Purpose

A website for the owner's own business whose success is measured by completed online purchases.

Open: the business name, what it sells (service, physical product, or digital product) and its market are not yet defined.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no real product photos, testimonials, customer logos, prices, reviews, or case studies in the repository. Future work must not fabricate any of them; use clearly marked placeholders until the owner supplies real material.

## Product Principles

1. Every surface serves the purchase: the path from arrival to paid order stays short and obvious.
2. Truth over filler: no invented claims, proof, or prices; placeholders are labeled as such.
3. Trust is earned at checkout: payment, pricing, and policies are clear before the customer commits.
